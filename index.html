Questions restaurant owners ask

Do my customers need to install anything?+

No. They message your WhatsApp number and tap the buttons that appear. Any phone with WhatsApp works.

How do I confirm an Easypaisa payment?+

The customer sends the 11-digit transaction ID. You check it in your Easypaisa account, then reply “PAID ORD-0012”, or “REJECT ORD-0012” if the money isn’t there. The customer is told either way.

What happens when we’re closed?+

The bot tells customers your opening hours instead of taking an order. You decide the hours, including late-night and Ramzan timings.

Which WhatsApp number do I use?+

A business number registered with WhatsApp’s business platform. We guide you through setup, and Meta’s business verification can take a few days.
